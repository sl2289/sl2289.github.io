I am a PhD candidate at Purdue University, advised by [Dr. Yingjie Victor Chen](https://polytechnic.purdue.edu/profile/chen489), specializing in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), Multimodal AI, and AI-augmented user experiences. My research explores how intelligent systems can better understand human intent, context, and behavior to enable more natural and effective human-AI interaction and collaboration.

My broader interests include human-centered AI, multimodal interaction, intelligent user interfaces, AI agents, Mixed Reality (MR), Extended Reality (XR), and emerging interaction paradigms. My work draws on UX research, user studies, experimental design, data analysis, interaction design, immersive systems, and rapid AI prototyping.
