I am a PhD candidate at Purdue University, advised by [Dr. Yingjie Victor Chen](https://polytechnic.purdue.edu/profile/chen489), specializing in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), Multimodal AI, and AI-augmented video-based software learning. 

My research focuses on **Human-AI Interaction** and **Multimodal Intelligent Interfaces**, exploring how AI systems can better understand users' intentions, behaviors, and contexts to provide intuitive and effective assistance in complex digital workflows. I combine empirical user research, interaction design, AI prototyping, and experimental evaluation to investigate and develop intelligent interactive systems.
